Add an optional sign-out after a period of inactivity

The interface refreshes its session for as long as the tab is open, so
an unattended browser stays signed in indefinitely (duplicati/duplicati#3369).

Add a "Sign out when idle" setting with a choice of 5 minutes to 4 hours.
It is stored per browser and is off by default, so nothing changes for
existing users. While it is on, pointer, key, wheel, touch and scroll
activity restarts the idle time, and the last activity is shared through
local storage so a tab left in the background does not sign out a
session that is in use in another tab. The check also runs when the tab
becomes visible again, because timers do not run while the machine
sleeps. Relay and proxy sessions are not signed in with the passphrase
and are left alone, as for the passphrase change sign-out.
